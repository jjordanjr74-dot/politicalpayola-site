Privacy Policy & Data Integrity

This Privacy Policy explains how Political Payola — Verified Multi-Source Civic Atlas (1981–2026) ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit https://politicalpayola.com/, query our WebMCP civic endpoints, or subscribe to The Payola Ledger or Pro research tiers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Zero Third-Party Ad Tracking: Political Payola accepts $0 from political candidates or corporate PACs and runs zero third-party behavioral ad networks.
  • Voluntary Subscriptions & Petitions: When you sign up for The Payola Ledger, sign a citizen petition, or purchase a Pro API/War-Room subscription via Stripe, we collect only the email address, state/district, and billing confirmation required to deliver your service.
  • Standard Infrastructure Logs: Anonymized HTTP access logs (browser type, timestamp, and page requested) processed via GitHub Pages / Cloudflare TLS for security and uptime.

2. Primary-Source Citation Standard

Every statistic, roll-call vote, campaign contribution, and judicial ruling published on Political Payola is cross-referenced against official primary public records (FEC.gov, Congress.gov, SupremeCourt.gov, the Congressional Budget Office, U.S. Census Bureau, and peer-reviewed medical/economic journals).

3. Payment Security (Stripe)

All memberships, API licenses, and custom research dossier orders are processed directly through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant Stripe Checkout. Political Payola never stores raw payment card numbers on our servers.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You may request export or deletion of any newsletter or account information at any time via the contact channels on politicalpayola.com.
