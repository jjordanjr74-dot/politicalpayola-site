How to read a filing

State portals publish the raw documents: contribution reports, expenditure reports and sometimes a searchable database. Look for three things — who gave, how much, and what the committee bought with it. Independent expenditure filings are where the big money usually hides, because they are not subject to the same contribution caps as direct donations.
